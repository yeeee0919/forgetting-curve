# toocheepfordutch — Buttons & Icons

Adapted from [claude DESIGN.md](https://github.com/VoltAgent/awesome-design-md/blob/main/design-md/claude/DESIGN.md) (VoltAgent/awesome-design-md). Keep Canal Ink colors; use Claude’s button and line-icon geometry.

## Buttons

- Type: 14px / 500, line-height 1
- Height: 40px
- Padding: 12px 20px
- Radius: 8px (`rounded.md`)
- Primary: coral fill (`--brand-accent`), white text. Active darkens. No heavy glow.
- Secondary: surface fill, ink text, 1px hairline (`--border-default`)
- Icon button: 36px circle, hairline, ink stroke icon

## Icons

- Line-art only. Stroke 1.75, round caps and joins.
- `currentColor`. Never mix emoji with stroke icons on the same surface.
- Sizes: 16px inside 40px buttons, 18px inside 36px icon buttons, 20px in tab bar.
