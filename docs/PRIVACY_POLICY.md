# Privacy Policy — Toocheep Word Catcher

**Effective / Last updated:** 2026-08-14  
**Product:** Toocheep Word Catcher (Chrome extension) and the toocheepfordutch web app  
**App URL:** https://forgetting-curve-ten.vercel.app/  
**Hosted policy URL (recommended for Chrome Web Store):** https://forgetting-curve-ten.vercel.app/privacy.html

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how **Toocheep Word Catcher** (“the Extension”) collects, uses, and protects your information.

The Extension provides a context-menu (and related in-page) workflow that lets you select text on a webpage and quickly save that word or phrase, together with relevant surrounding context, into your personal vocabulary inbox for review in [toocheepfordutch](https://forgetting-curve-ten.vercel.app/).

By using the Extension, you acknowledge this Policy. If you do not agree, please disable or uninstall the Extension.

---

## 2. Data We Collect

We collect data **only when you take an explicit action** (select text and use the context menu or UI to save it to your vocabulary inbox):

| Category (Chrome Web Store) | What we collect |
|---|---|
| **Website content** | The text you select, and related sentence context needed to understand meaning. |
| **Web history (limited)** | The source page URL for that save (for provenance). We do **not** record a full browsing history, visit timeline, or pages you never saved from. |
| **Optional translation** | A translation of the selected text, if translation is requested to display results and store with the inbox item. |

We do **not** collect: name, email, phone number, government ID, health or financial data, passwords or authentication secrets, private messages, precise location, or continuous monitoring of clicks, keystrokes, scrolling, or mouse position.

---

## 3. How We Use Data

Collected data is used solely to:

1. Provide the core “add selection to vocabulary inbox” feature;  
2. Display, manage, and review saved items in the learning app;  
3. Show translations of selected text when that feature is enabled.

We do **not** use this data to track or profile your general browsing behavior, and we do **not** sell it or transfer it to third parties for advertising or unrelated purposes.

---

## 4. Transfer, Storage, and Security

- Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (**HTTPS**).  
- To provide sync/inbox features, data may be stored in our cloud database provider (e.g. Supabase) and/or in your browser’s local storage.  
- Translation requests may be sent to a third-party translation service; only the text needed for translation is processed.  
- We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures and limit access to what is necessary for the core feature.

Please avoid selecting sensitive personal information. You may delete inbox/card items in the app at any time, or disable/uninstall the Extension.

---

## 5. Sharing

We do not share your saved selections except:

- with infrastructure or translation providers **as necessary** to operate the core feature; or  
- when required by law or to protect rights and safety.

---

## 6. Children’s Privacy

The Extension is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 7. Changes

We may update this Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you are aware of the revised Policy.

### 1. 前言
本隱私權政策說明「Toocheep Word Catcher」如何收集、使用與保護您的資料。本擴充功能提供右鍵選單功能，讓您在網頁選取文字後，將單字／句子與相關上下文加入個人單字庫（Inbox），以便於 toocheepfordutch 複習。使用本擴充功能即表示您了解本政策；若不同意，請停用或移除。

### 2. 資料收集範圍
僅在您**主動**選取並執行收集／加入單字庫時收集：
- **網站內容**：選取的文字與相關上下文句子  
- **網頁記錄（有限）**：該次收集的來源網頁網址（非完整瀏覽紀錄）  
- **譯文（選擇性）**：所選文字之翻譯結果  

不收集姓名、Email、健康／財務資料、密碼、私人通訊、精確定位，也不在背景監控點擊或鍵盤輸入。

### 3. 資料使用目的
僅用於實現「加入單字庫」、在 App 中顯示／複習，以及（若啟用）顯示翻譯。不用於行為追蹤或廣告，**不販售**，亦不為無關目的轉移給第三方。

### 4. 資料保護
以 HTTPS 傳輸；可能儲存於雲端資料庫（如 Supabase）及／或本機瀏覽器儲存空間；翻譯可能經第三方翻譯服務處理。您可隨時刪除 Inbox 內容或解除安裝擴充功能。
